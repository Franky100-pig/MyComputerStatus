Four consecutive records, each ~1.4–2.4× the last, with `pageouts` still under 1 GiB. Reads, not

per-day trend inherits that noise.

### 6.2 The sweep found one thing

| Sweep | Result |
|---|---|
| `find ~ -xdev -size +300M -newermt <interval start>` | **1 file** — a **740 MB installer**, written 23:19 on 11-20 |
| `find ~ -xdev -size +1M -newermt <interval start>` | **361 files / 2 421 MB** |

So the home directory accounted for ~2.4 GB of the +6.6 GB, of which the installer is the single

The 740 MB installer is also the most likely explanation for the 23:28 swap burst (§5.2) and for
the third-party microstackshot in §7.3: a large download, an installer, a new app launching.

### 6.3 ⭐ The 15 GB update vanished from its volume and took no space with it

Yesterday's prediction, quoted from this log: *"if the update installs, expect a jump upward"* —
free space should have returned to ~149 GB. What actually happened:

| Evidence | Value |
|---|---|
| `/System/Volumes/Update` used | **966 MB** (was 15 GB) |
| Free space | **127.3 GB** — down 6.6 GB, not up 15 |
| `restore.log` in that volume | **2.8 MB, written 10:44** |
| `softwareupdated` at 10:44:40 | `DO MSU BACKGROUND ACTIONS!` … `CurrentFSMState: Prepared`, `msuPrepareSize: 13 129 211 278` |
| `tmutil listlocalsnapshots /` | **3** `com.apple.os.update-*` snapshots (was 1) |
| Boot epoch | **unchanged** — no restart |

The coherent reading: at 10:44 the update daemon re-ran its prepare phase and wrote a **fresh
`com.apple.os.update-MSUPrepareUpdate` snapshot on `/`**. The payload is no longer a file tree on
the Update volume; it is pinned inside the volume-group snapshots. `df` counts snapshot blocks
as used but cannot free them, which is exactly why free space fell by the same 6.6 GB the Data
volume grew and **not** by 15 GB less.

That also means the 10.4 GB that went missing on 11-20 (used +5.8, free −16.1) was never
contradictory — it was the same mechanism, seen while the volume still held the files.

**The update still has not installed.** Uptime is 14.6 days, the boot epoch is untouched, and
`AutomaticallyInstallMacOSUpdates` remains `0`. The machine is carrying a fully prepared
13.1 GB macOS 26.7.3 update and will keep carrying it until someone clicks install.

### 8.4 Pitfalls re-confirmed, and two new ones

| Trap | Status today |
|---|---|
| `log show --end` without seconds | avoided — every query used full `HH:MM:SS` |
