**"Free = 0.05 GB" is not a problem and never has been on this machine** — macOS deliberately
keeps free pages near zero and serves allocations from inactive/purgeable memory instead. The
meaningful number is inactive at **4.56 GB**, which is cached-but-reclaimable, and it is what

occupied 3.48 GB** — the ratio worth remembering: the compressor is *holding* 10.2 GB of
logically-compressed material in 3.48 GB of real RAM, a 2.9× saving. That is why the compressor
number rising is usually good news rather than bad.
