   contradicts both raw gauges, which rose. If it holds through 11-11 it is real wear; if it
   returns to 90 % it was rounding. Either way, **stop calling `NominalChargeCapacity` "the

5. **`memorystatus` record counts need an awake-time denominator** (§4.3). 185 vs 1 350 is 93 %

6. **Sample the battery in the evening, once.** Two consecutive 30–47 point daytime discharges
   have happened entirely outside every sample this log owns (§6.4). Without one evening reading
   the daily series will keep reporting "a few points" for a machine that is doing most of its
   cycling unseen.

printed. Process categories are genericised ("a system content-indexing daemon", "an antivirus
framework"). All per-24-h figures are **uptime-normalised or interval-derived**, never
extrapolated from one instantaneous sample.
