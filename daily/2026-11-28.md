## 8. Repository changes, the CSV row, and what this run corrected

### 8.1 🟢 No schema change — and one key that would have paid for itself

`DIAG_STORE` reads `present`, which is correct and unhelpful: both stores exist, both are 30–45
minutes old, and the number that matters is the birth time, which the key does not carry. A
`DIAG_BIRTH_H` column would have said "0.75 h" instead of "present" and made §1.2's argument
without prose. Not added today — the CSV's shape is worth more than the convenience — but
recorded as a candidate.

### 8.2 🟡 README: four claims retired, one added

1. **The `.diag` burst is not plug-triggered.** §1.3 has the null case: eight payloads, zero power
   transitions. The four-day correlation is now presented as a coincidence, alongside the wake
   trigger it already documented.
2. **The 80–85 band is a limited-state property.** §3.3: at 100 % with the limit off, the pack
   held a flat **21 h 25 m** with no records at all, and the drift went into `StateOfCharge` (97)
   instead of the published level. A flat 85 is hysteresis; a flat 100 is charging disabled.
3. **A `per-process-limit` kill can lose its `.ips`.** The kernel line survives a store rebuild;
   the report does not. `JETSAM_24H` should be read as "reports that outlived the store", which
   on a day with two store rebuilds is close to meaningless.
4. **New: a boot epoch cannot drift.** Today's register reads `1793971472` = 2026-11-06 21:24:32;
   reports on 11-18 and 11-19/11-26 quoted `1793971474` and `1793971475`. Uptime rose by exactly
   1.0 d across those runs and no reboot appears in any stream, so those values were derived
   rather than read. `sysctl -n kern.boottime` is the only correct source, and the 3 seconds are
   a reminder that a derived timestamp with the right number of digits looks exactly like a
   register read.
5. **`charge_rate_max` does not need a plug event — corrected.** The README said a machine plugged
   in for the whole interval has no AC segment and therefore reads `0.0`. Today is that machine:
   `ac_plugs = 0`, 24.2 h AC, and the column read **16.3** from a clean 55 → 100 % ramp inside the
   single whole-window segment. The real `0.0` condition is a segment with **no net rise**, which
   is why 11-22 also read `0.0` — it refilled 80 → 85, back to the band it started in.

### 8.5 Pitfalls re-confirmed

- **Count `killing (idle)` / `killing_specific_process`, not `killing`.** 102 kills versus 202
  lines (§5.4).
- **`JETSAM_24H` is a count of survivors, not of events.** Two `per-process-limit` kills, zero
  `.ips` (§1.2).
- **`stat -f %SB` on both diagnostic stores before quoting a zero.** Both were rebuilt today,
  one of them twice within 16 minutes.
- **A four-sample correlation is not a mechanism.** The plug→burst link died on the first day
  with no plug; the six-day charge cadence died on the first day after it was proposed.
- **`AC_PLUGS = 0` needs `time_ac_h`.** 24.2 h today, 16.5 h on battery on 11-23 (§3.2).
- **A flat charge level means nothing without the limit state.** 21 h 25 m at 100 is charging
  disabled; 86 min at 85 is a limit (§3.3).
- **The sampler is not the snapshot.** Mean 10.79 GB against 11.14 GB at capture, eight minutes
  apart.
- **A boot epoch read from a derived number looks like a register read.** §8.2.
