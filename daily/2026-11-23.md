## 8. Repository changes, the CSV row, and one bug we fixed

### 8.1 ⭐ `collect_charging.py` mis-labelled the whole interval as battery

The pre-first-transition segment was seeded from `curve[0]`, the **first capacity record**. On a
held charge there are no capacity records until something changes — so today that record was the
23:20:24 *detach* itself, carrying `Source:Batt`. The 9.4 hours on AC before it inherited the
post-detach label:

```
before:  TIME_AC_H=0.0   TIME_BATT_H=25.9   (wrong: 25.9 h is the whole interval)
after :  TIME_AC_H=9.4   TIME_BATT_H=16.5   (sum = 25.9 h, matches the 23:20:24 transition)
```

A transition to X means the machine was **not** on X immediately before it, so when no capacity
record predates the first transition, the collector now inverts that transition's source. A
second defect surfaced while testing: an `--until` earlier than a transition produced a
backwards segment and a **negative** `TIME_BATT_H`; segments are now clipped to the window.

Regression checks on a saved 26 h dump:

| Window | `TIME_AC_H` | `TIME_BATT_H` |
|---|---|---|
| Full 25.92 h | 9.4 | 16.5 |
| 13:54 → 23:00 (before the detach) | 9.1 | 0.0 |
| 00:00 → 15:50 (after the detach) | 0.0 | 15.8 |
| 13:54 → 17:00 (short AC-only) | 3.1 | 0.0 |

Days whose first capacity record predates their first transition are unaffected — the new branch
only fires when the record is at or after the transition. Yesterday's all-AC case has no
transition at all and takes the old path.

### 8.4 Pitfalls re-confirmed, and two new ones

Re-confirmed: use `/usr/bin/log`, never bare `log` (the shell builtin shadows it); check
`pmset` retention first; `du -sh` on the Update volume is meaningless; the same `powerd` state
post is emitted twice; `Charge:` values from `pmset` are not to be trusted.

New: (1) a charge segment cannot be seeded from the first capacity record — a held charge
produces none until the transition itself; (2) `diskutil apfs listSnapshots <Data volume>` misses
volume-group snapshots — use `tmutil listlocalsnapshots /`.
